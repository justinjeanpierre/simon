What was the purpose of building this tool?

The goal is to provide Graphite, an open-source, distributed parallel simulator for multicore architectures developed by MIT as a service. This makes Computer Architects and Engineers spend their time on design instead of software configuration and development.

Why the choice of Graphite over other simulators?

One of the main reasons behind this choice is that Graphite is capable of accelerating simulations by distributing them across multiple commodity Linux machines.

Are there any restrictions on the usage?

Graphite is open-source under the MIT license, hence, we follow the same philosophy. Permission is hereby granted, free of charge, to any person to use this application and associated documentation files (the "Software"), to deal in the Software without restriction and for any purposes of use governed by the law.

Is this the final state of the project?

No, the project is still in development. We plan on expanding to have diff modes of running jobs i.e. basic and advanced making the designed processors more configurable. Finally, we also plan on expanding on the choice of application to have more than just four.

Are there limitations to what can be done?

As of now, paying for compute resources is expensive and any donations could help. If interested, email us. Donation money would especially allow us to improve the performance in the future. Thank you in advance =]
